### 2.2 Core Engine Pillars (6 Canonical Modules)
1. **High-Speed POS & Billing:** Fast barcode checkout, multi-tender split (Cash, Card, bKash, Nagad), till cash drawer float balancing, thermal receipt printing.
2. **FEFO Batch & Expiry Matrix:** Oldest-expiry automated dispatch, weighing scale EAN-13 barcodes, 30/60/90-day expiry radar, low-stock reorder drafts.
3. **Double-Entry Ledgers & Net VAT:** Balanced journal entries posted at receipt generation, accounts receivable aging, NBR Net VAT 9.1 returns.
4. **Multi-Store & Gate Pass Transfers:** Central warehouse inwarding, in-transit gate pass tracking, inter-branch replenishment, unified P&L.
5. **Real-Time Profit Intelligence:** Gross margin analytics, fast-moving vs slow-moving stock matrix, cashier performance audits.
6. **Enterprise Architecture & Trust:** Row-level multi-tenant isolation, automated cloud backups, tamper-evident audit logs.
